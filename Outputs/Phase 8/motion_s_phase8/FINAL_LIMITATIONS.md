# Final limitations

1. The 668-D semantics are only partially verified.
2. No verified feature-to-joints inverse is available.
3. No generated BVH is produced.
4. MPJPE is not calculated.
5. No human or sign-recognition evaluation was performed.
6. Continuous feature similarity is not equivalent to linguistic sign correctness.
7. Collapse remains measurable.
8. Phase-3 errors affect predicted length and motion generation.
9. Test data was evaluated in multiple development phases and is not a pristine once-only holdout.
10. Further architectural changes are future work.
